## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an institutional yet forward-leaning atmosphere tailored for high-stakes municipal, cooperative, and commercial infrastructure investments. Housing Society (RWA) board members require utmost clarity, structural auditability, and fiscal transparency, while solar EPC contractors demand dense, high-velocity operational workflows and real-time bidding clarity.

The aesthetic philosophy fuses **High-Precision Minimalist FinTech** with **Luminous Glassmorphism**. Dark slate foundations supply a calm, focused trading environment, offset by high-luminance glass surfaces, crystalline micro-borders, and targeted spectral accents:
- **Clean Architectural Discipline:** Rigorous alignment, crisp boundary definitions, and mathematical typography evoke the permanence of civil engineering and financial underwriting.
- **Controlled Luminescence:** Vital metrics, active bids, and power generation telemetry emit controlled ambient light through translucent structural cards without introducing visual noise.
- **Institutional Weight:** Components prioritize dense spatial legibility, eliminating decorative whimsy in favor of dense data tables, verified credential badges, and deterministic status indicators.

## Colors

The palette balances energy generation metrics, competitive bid mechanics, and regulatory compliance:

- **Primary (`#10B981` Emerald Green):** Represents clean power generation, approved tenders, verified ESG savings, and primary transactional actions. Use `#059669` for hover, active states, and dense inline badges requiring elevated contrast.
- **Secondary (`#F59E0B` Solar Amber):** Reserved strictly for financial yield returns, internal rates of return (IRR), blind-auction countdowns, and active bidding spreads. Deepens to `#D97706` for focus states and boundary indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#0EA5E9` Electric Sky):** Signifies technical telemetry, grid interconnection status, irradiance sensors, and auxiliary network actions.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#0F172A` Deep Midnight Slate):** Serves as the bedrock foundation, establishing depth against `#0B0F19` backdrop voids, `#1E293B` structural strokes, and high-luminance `#FFFFFF` floating data cards with variable opacity (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)` through `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).

## Typography

The typographic system utilizes a tri-font hierarchy to communicate institutional trust, clear editorial guidance, and granular numeric accuracy:

- **Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Employs geometric openness with modern warmth. Tight tracking on display sizes produces an authoritative executive appearance across portfolio dashboards and tender overviews.
- **Body & Controls (Inter):** The workhorse for all contractual legibility, legal RFP clauses, committee comments, and form controls. Provides tall x-height and neutral transparency.
- **Data & Telemetry (JetBrains Mono):** Reserved for financial bids, kWp capacity figures, rooftop coordinates, countdown timers, and tender verification hashes. Monospaced tracking ensures jitter-free updates during real-time blind-auction ticks.

## Layout & Spacing

The viewport layout enforces a rigid 12-column responsive fluid grid anchored by structural bounding coordinates:

- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column grid, `gutter: 1.5rem`, `margin: 2rem`, capped at a max-width of `1600px` for high-density control centers.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column grid, `gutter: 1.25rem`, `margin: 1.5rem`. Secondary analytic sidebars collapse into toggleable sheets.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid, `gutter-mobile: 1rem`, `margin-mobile: 1rem`. Bid tables reflow into unified vertical credential cards.

Subtle 32px x 32px technical grid lines (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)`) can be rendered across the neutral baseline canvas to reference rooftop engineering schematics.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on heavy drop shadows. It uses a disciplined composite of translucent surface layering, light refraction, and subtle boundary strokes:

- **Base Void (`#0B0F19`):** Ground level. Zero elevation. Host to subtle ambient radial gradients in emerald or amber (`opacity: 0.08`, `blur: 140px`).
- **Level 1 (Structural Canvas - `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.75)`):** Applied to persistent navigation sidebars, bidding consoles, and section wrappers. Features `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` with a hairline stroke: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Marketplace Cards - `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.65)`):** Applied to individual RWA tender listings, live bid tickets, and telemetry charts. Uses `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`, a top highlight stroke of `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, and lateral/bottom strokes of `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Overlays & Modals - `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.92)`):** High-priority blind-bidding submission dialogs and credential verification drawers. Employs `backdrop-filter: blur(32px)` combined with an ambient contact shadow: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)` and an internal luminous rim (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`).

## Shapes

The design system standardizes on **Rounded (Level 2)** geometry to balance technical precision with modern SaaS approachability:

- **Micro Controls & Badges (0.375rem / 6px):** Compact elements such as verification pills, table tags, and inline metric chips.
- **Default Elements (0.5rem / 8px):** Standard inputs, primary buttons, dropdown triggers, and modal action bars.
- **Cards & Data Containers (`rounded-lg`, 1rem / 16px):** Tender proposal cards, telemetry graphs, and live-bidding matrix blocks.
- **Drawers & Overlay Portals (`rounded-xl`, 1.5rem / 24px):** High-level operational modals and slide-over EPC qualification dossiers.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Triggers
- **Primary Auction Action:** Vibrant Emerald (`#10B981`), foreground deep navy (`#064E3B` / `#0F172A`), font-weight 600. Luminous hover state triggers a refined drop glow: `0 0 20px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.35)`.
- **Secondary Financial Trigger:** Frosted glass with subtle Solar Amber border (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.4)`), text `#FBBF24`, background `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.08)`.
- **Tertiary / Neutral Action:** Solid deep slate fill (`#1E293B`), text `#E2E8F0`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.

### Blind-Bidding Inputs & Fields
- Dark translucent fill (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.6)`) framed with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
- Focused state transitions border directly to primary emerald (`#10B981`) or secondary amber (`#F59E0B`) with an exterior field halo: `0 0 0 3px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)`.
- Numeric input surfaces always render values in `JetBrains Mono` with fixed units (e.g., `₹/kWh`, `kWp`) pinned as non-editable muted suffixes.

### Trust Badges & EPC Certifications
- **RWA Verification Stamp:** Double-ring pill badge consisting of an outer `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.2)` border, `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.08)` surface, active green pulsating indicator dot, and uppercase micro typography.
- **Tier-1 EPC Qualified:** Solar Amber tinted tag (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.15)`), micro icon anchor, indicating verified bank guarantees and past grid execution records.

### Marketplace Tender Cards
- Built using Level 2 glass surfaces (`backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`).
- Header zone houses the society identity, verified capacity stamp, and blind-bidding status countdown in amber monospace.
- Body displays a 3-column split metric grid: Usable Roof Area (sq. ft.), Target Capacity (kWp), and Estimated 25-Year Society Yield.
- Bottom actions feature full-width crisp dividers (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`) leading to sealed-bid submission paths.

### Data Tables & Blind-Auction Spread
- Dense, compact row geometry (`48px` row height) with alternating subtle row fills (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.015)`).
- Sealed competitor values are obscured with an animated frosted masking pattern and cryptographic hash indicators to ensure integrity.
